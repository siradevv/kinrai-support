Why does KinRai show a range?

A photo can’t show hidden oil, sugar or exactly how much rice is on the plate. KinRai gives the most likely number and an honest range, and asks only about what the camera can’t see. Answering narrows the range.

A meal is wrong. How do I fix it?

On the results page, just say what’s different, in Thai or English, like “half the rice” or “ไม่ได้กินน้ำซุป”. You can also tap an item to change its amount, swap it or remove it.

What about packaged food?

Take a photo of the nutrition label. KinRai copies the printed numbers exactly, then asks how much you ate.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if iCloud is on. There’s no account and no copy on our server. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I stop meals going to AI?

Settings › Work out meals with AI. KinRai then asks before your next meal.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Settings › Apple Health › Connect. KinRai only reads, for example weigh-ins from a smart scale, and never writes to Apple Health.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app, then on your iPhone open Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or Storage) › KinRai and delete its data.

Is this medical advice?

No. KinRai gives estimates, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have had an eating disorder, talk to a doctor before changing how you eat.
