Privacy Policy

KinRai · Last updated: 3 November 2026

KinRai has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your meals, body details and weigh-ins are kept on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. To work out what’s in a meal, KinRai sends the meal to an AI service, and only after you allow it. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we are

KinRai is made by an independent developer in Thailand. Questions about privacy go to sira.dev@hotmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud

Your profile (age, sex, height, weight, goal, activity and daily targets), your meals and meal photos, weigh-ins you type in, your usual meals and your past daily targets are kept in the app. If iCloud is on, they are also kept in your private iCloud database, so they come back when you reinstall or change iPhones. Only your Apple Account can open that database; we can’t see it.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, KinRai reads your weight, body fat, lean mass, waist, height, date of birth, sex, steps, active calories, workouts, sleep, resting heart rate and cardio fitness. KinRai only reads; it never writes to Apple Health.

The readings themselves (each weigh-in, step count, night of sleep and so on) stay on your iPhone. They are never stored in iCloud, never sent with your meals, and never used for advertising or marketing. They are used to show your numbers on the Health tab and to keep your targets up to date. Your profile’s current weight follows your weight trend, which can include weigh-ins from Apple Health, and is kept with your profile. Nothing else from Apple Health leaves your iPhone, and none of it is sent to the AI services, including for weekly tips.

Meals and AI

When you add a meal with photos or words, KinRai sends our server what you added: up to three meal photos, nutrition label photos, what you type about the meal, and, for “Snap what’s left”, the photos of the meal before and after. Our server passes them to Google Gemini to work out what’s in the meal. If Gemini is busy, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI does it instead. Your body details and Apple Health data are never sent with your meals.

Our server doesn’t keep your photos or what you type. It records technical details only: which feature was used, which AI model answered, how long it took and what it cost. The AI companies handle the data under their business (API) terms: they don’t use it to train their AI, and they may keep it for a limited time to watch for abuse. Their policies: Google Gemini API, Anthropic, OpenAI.

KinRai asks before the first meal is sent. You can turn this off any time in Settings › Work out meals with AI; KinRai then asks again before your next meal.

You can also add meals without AI. Picking foods from KinRai’s list sends only the foods and amounts to our server, which works out the numbers from its food data; nothing goes to the AI companies. Typed calories stay on your iPhone.

Weekly tips

Weekly tips are worked out on your iPhone. If you turn on AI wording, KinRai sends a short summary to the same AI services so they can word the tips: your goal, your language and a few numbers about your meals, such as your average protein or fibre and how many days you logged. Tips about weight, body or activity are always worded on your iPhone, so nothing from Apple Health is sent. Never photos or meals. You can turn this off in Settings › Weekly tips.

Meal photos in your library

“Find meals in my photos” is off unless you turn it on, and needs access to all your photos. It looks at new photos taken with the Camera app using Apple’s on-device image recognition. Nothing is sent anywhere until you tap + on a meal it found; that meal is then sent as described in “Meals and AI”.

Camera and photos

KinRai uses the camera and photos only for the meals and labels you choose to add.

Location

If you allow it, KinRai uses your approximate location (your town, not your street) to show the weather behind the Today page. It is sent only to Apple’s weather service, never to us, and isn’t stored. Without it, Today shows a sky for the time of day.

Anonymous usage data

KinRai sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, such as “a meal was logged” or “onboarding was finished”, with the app version, device type and language. They use an anonymous ID and never include your meals, photos, what you type, your body details or Health data. You can turn this off in Settings › Share anonymous usage data.

Device ID and hosting

The app makes a random ID for your device so our server can limit how many requests one device sends in an hour. It isn’t linked to your name or Apple Account. Our server runs on Railway, which keeps short-term technical logs that can include network details such as an IP address.

KinRai Pro purchases

KinRai Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; KinRai never sees your card or payment details, only whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in KinRai’s Settings or in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

What we don’t do

No accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps and websites, no selling or sharing of your data for marketing.

Deleting your data

You can delete any meal or weigh-in in the app. To delete everything, delete the app, then on your iPhone open Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or Storage) › KinRai and delete its data. We keep no copy of your meals or profile on our server, so there is nothing else to delete. Email us if you have any questions.

Your rights

Under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and similar laws, you can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your permission at any time using the switches in Settings. Email sira.dev@hotmail.com.

Age

KinRai is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Not medical advice

KinRai gives estimates, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have had an eating disorder, talk to a doctor before changing how you eat.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
